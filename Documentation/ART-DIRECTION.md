# Visual direction v12

A temple reclaimed by nature: cool stone and distance, warm fires, and blue or red Force energy used sparingly. Architecture and light guide the player toward the gate. Low cover and side paths vary the combat space. Trees, scanned rock and textures are actual scene assets rather than a flat background replacing game geometry.

Side selection occupies the entire screen. Black dims the inactive half. Selection does not depend on color alone: the active side has brighter text and a confirmation button. Portraits are animated models rendered from the game. The main menu contains the title and actions. Avoid overlines, slogans, page numbering and version labels. The HUD shows resources, abilities and essential instructions only. All interface copy is English.

The opening introduces the actual playable location rather than an unrelated pre-rendered landscape. Four deliberate shots establish the valley, mentor, hero and playable viewpoint. A brief fade conceals each camera cut; the last shot ends at the exact shoulder-camera pose. Letterboxing, short English subtitles and a quiet ambient bed distinguish the sequence without adding decorative UI. The intro remains skippable with either input scheme.

Combat follows anticipation, active cut and recovery. Damage is synchronized with the cut. Authored animation clips drive the hands and body. Restrained movement assistance, short hit stop, enemy reactions and a limited blade trail make contact readable. Clothing changes must not override weapon colors.

References:

- [Quaternius Universal Animation Library 2](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2): authored sword sequences with separate recoveries, compatible humanoid rigs and in-place variants.
- [Game Accessibility Guidelines — UI contrast](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/provide-high-contrast-between-text-ui-and-background/): readable text against changing backgrounds.
- [Making Color Usage Accessible](https://www.section508.gov/create/making-color-usage-accessible/): information must not rely solely on color recognition.

The project retains the working Force powers, directional locomotion, tutorial/wave/boss structure and controller support from the previous implementation. The [project README](../README.md) describes the current build and active animations.
